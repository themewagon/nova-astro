# Nova, content guide

Nova is a one-page creative agency template. The default copy is demonstration content and must be replaced before production use.

## Content rules

- Replace the company name, contact details and social links.
- Replace all demonstration numbers, testimonials and project names.
- Replace every image with a properly licensed asset.
- Keep one clear H1 on the homepage.
- Keep headings specific to the target brand.
- Keep CTA labels action-oriented.
- Keep internal URLs with trailing slashes.

## Active sections

The homepage currently uses navigation, hero, selected projects, services, team and values, FAQ, testimonials and legal links in the footer.

The section order is defined in `src/data/pages/index.json`.

## Language versions

Nova includes Polish and English copy. Update both language objects in `src/data/i18n/nova.json` when changing the homepage.

Before publishing, confirm that both versions contain equivalent information and working links.
